Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AgentSite Control Plane and the related Hermes Personal Assistant SMS workflow handle information.

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Overview

AgentSite Control Plane is a public demonstration website for safe AI-maintained landing pages. Hermes Personal Assistant is a private assistant workflow that may use SMS to send alerts, reminders, status updates, and conversational replies to authorized users.

Information we collect

  • Website visit information that may be collected by GitHub Pages or the visitor's browser, such as basic request metadata.
  • SMS information when an authorized user texts the service or receives a message, including phone number, message body, timestamps, delivery status, and carrier/Twilio metadata.
  • Operational logs needed to debug delivery, abuse prevention, reliability, and assistant behavior.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the website, deliver requested SMS messages, respond to inbound messages, troubleshoot failures, maintain security, and improve reliability of the private assistant workflow.

SMS consent and sharing

SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS data may be processed by service providers, including Twilio, only as needed to deliver and operate messaging.

Opt out and help

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or visit the SMS information page.

Retention

SMS and operational records are retained only as long as needed for delivery, troubleshooting, security, compliance, or normal assistant operation, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the public GitHub repository contact path: https://github.com/castiliad/agentsite-control-plane.
