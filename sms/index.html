SMS program

Hermes Personal Assistant SMS

SMS is used for private assistant messages such as alerts, reminders, status updates, and conversational replies to authorized users.

Opt-in

Users opt in by texting the service number directly or by being manually configured as an authorized recipient for the private Hermes Personal Assistant workflow. This page, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms of Service describe the messaging program.

Message types

  • Assistant replies to inbound SMS messages.
  • Personal reminders and requested notifications.
  • Operational alerts, status updates, and monitoring notifications.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. After opting out, you may receive a final confirmation message and then no further SMS messages unless you opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for help. You can also review this page for messaging details and use the GitHub repository contact path: https://github.com/castiliad/agentsite-control-plane.

Privacy note

SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Sample messages

  • Hermes: Reminder — leave in 15 minutes for your appointment. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Hermes: The home assistant gateway is offline. I’ll retry monitoring in 5 minutes. Reply HELP for help.
  • Hermes: Got it. I’ll summarize that note and save it for later.
